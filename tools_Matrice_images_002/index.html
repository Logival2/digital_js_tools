Cards

No image: 50 blank cards. Several at once, or drop them on the page.

Mode

Environment

Camera

Off. Uses your camera, analysed only in this browser.

Controls

Grid: drag or scroll to move, click a card, arrows to step, Esc to leave. Space: drag to move, scroll to fly forward or back, click a card to reach it. Sphere and Mont Blanc: drag, scroll, double-click to recentre. H hides the panel, F full screen.

Privacy · GDPR

Data controller

Valentin Maynadié — contact: valentinmaynadie@gmail.com

Images

The images you add are read and drawn inside your browser. They are never uploaded or stored, and disappear when you close the page.

Camera

The camera only turns on when you click “Start camera”, after your browser asks for permission. The video stream is analysed only in your browser, to find the position of your hand, and shown in a small black and white preview: no image or video is recorded, kept or sent to a server. You can turn it off at any time with “Stop camera”.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no user accounts. Fonts and terrain data are served with the page.

Third-party services

Only if you turn on hand control, your browser downloads files from:

  • jsDelivr — the hand tracking library (MediaPipe);
  • Google Cloud Storage — the hand tracking model.

These services receive your IP address, which they need to send the files, under their own privacy policies.

Terrain data

Mont Blanc relief: Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (Mapzen / Tilezen), from SRTM (NASA), EU-DEM (Copernicus) and other open sources, reduced to a 140 m grid.

Your rights

This site keeps no personal data. For any question about your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection), write to the address above. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
