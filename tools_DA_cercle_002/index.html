    Privacy · GDPR

    Data controller

    Valentin Maynadié — contact: valentinmaynadie@gmail.com

    Camera

    The camera only turns on when you click “Start camera”, after your browser asks for permission. The video stream is analysed only in your browser, to find the position of your hand, and shown in a small black and white preview: no image or video is recorded, kept or sent to a server. You can turn it off at any time with “Stop camera”.

    Images and exports

    The images you import stay on your device: they are loaded in memory for the session and disappear when you close the page. PNG and MP4 files are made locally.

    Cookies and tracking

    This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no user accounts.

    Third-party services

    To work, your browser downloads files from:

    • jsDelivr — the hand tracking library (MediaPipe), loaded when the camera starts, and, for MP4 exports, the video muxer;
    • Google Cloud Storage — hand tracking model.

    These services receive your IP address, which they need to send the files, under their own privacy policies.

    Your rights

    This site keeps no personal data. For any question about your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection), write to the address above. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
