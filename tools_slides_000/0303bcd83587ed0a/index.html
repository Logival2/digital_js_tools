Slides

Export your Figma slides as PNG, then drop them on the page, with videos (MP4, WebM) if you like. Sorted by file name: 01.png, 02.mp4… Then drag the cards to reorder them, or to the “Set aside” column to keep them out of the deck.

Live web

The site runs for real in the slide: click, type, use the webcam. Some sites refuse to be framed: their slide keeps an “Open in a new tab” button.
