Mode

Cards

No image: 50 black cards. Several at once, or drop them on the page.

Motion

Grid: drag or scroll to move, click a card, arrows to step, Esc to leave. Sphere, space and Mont Blanc: drag, scroll, double-click to recentre. H hides the panel, F full screen.

Terrain data

Mont Blanc relief: Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (Mapzen / Tilezen), from SRTM (NASA), EU-DEM (Copernicus) and other open sources, reduced to a 140 m grid.
