Camera

The camera only turns on when you click “Start camera”, after your browser asks for permission. The video stream is analysed only in your browser, to cut out your silhouette: no image or video is recorded, kept or sent to a server. A loaded MP4 video also stays on your computer.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no user accounts.

Third-party services

  • jsDelivr — body cutout (MediaPipe), loaded when the camera starts;
  • Google Cloud Storage — cutout model.

These services receive your IP address, which they need to send the files.
